The problem

About 9 in 10 eligible small businesses never claimed the money.

$15 billion in federal small-business tax credits was available for tax year 2011. Owners want the money. Finding it is tedious: countless forms, scattered rules, no place to start — and no one tracking what happens after you send.

MoneyFinder turns that paperwork into one guided flow: learn the business, cite the program, fill the official form, get your approval, and track the application.

How it works

Three steps. One agent. You approve the money moments.

01

Connect your inbox

Read-only synthetic mailbox. The agent finds payroll, hires, equipment and receipts, then builds a profile you can edit. Every fact cites the email it came from.

02

See what you qualify for

Public-agency programs matched to your facts with a plain-language “why this fits you” and a citation. Expired programs are filtered out, not filed.

03

Fill, approve, track

The agent pulls the official PDF and fills every field from your profile. You review and approve; then the application moves from Found to In process, Approved, and Paid.

The money rule

Identified is not approved.
Approved is not paid.

A match is an estimate. An approval is agency evidence. Paid means a deposit matched to the application. MoneyFinder never blurs the three — and never calls a form “filed” until you approve it.
